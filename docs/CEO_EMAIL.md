## 1. Email to the CEO

**Subject:** ROAR Mobile website: ready for your review, and the details I need from you

Hi [CEO NAME],

The new ROAR Mobile website is built and ready for you to look at: [PREVIEW LINK]

Please open it on your phone first, since that is how most visitors will see it. It is available in English and Spanish (tap "Español" in the menu).

It is designed to be honest about anything not yet confirmed. Where I do not have a confirmed fact, the site says "will be published here" instead of showing a guess. So there are no wrong numbers on it, but it also means a few sections look empty until you answer the questions below. As soon as you reply, I can fill them in.

**What I need from you**

Short answers are fine. You can reply to this email with the number and your answer, for example "1: $160,000".

**A. The money (the most important part)**

1. **What is the official total project cost?** The current website shows three different figures: $150,000–$165,000 on the English homepage, $160,000–$195,000 on the Spanish homepage, and $160,000–$175,000 on the Spanish Mobile Unit page. I need one number, or one range, and I will use only that everywhere.
2. Is the first fundraising goal still **$100,000 to begin construction**?
3. Do you want to show **how much has been raised so far**? If yes, what is the amount now, and who will update it and how often?
4. Is this sentence still accurate: **"100% of funds are restricted to building ROAR Mobile"**?
5. May we add this note on the Mobile Unit page: **"The project budget is subject to change as equipment, construction, and operating requirements are finalized"**?

**B. The Founding 250 membership**

6. Is it still **$25 a month for 12 months, or $300 a year**?
7. **How many Founding Members are there right now?** Do you want that number shown on the site as a progress bar ("87 of 250")?
8. What exactly do members receive? The site currently says: a ROAR Mobile shirt or tank, a Founding Member card, a monthly local-business perk, and monthly progress updates. Is that right, and is any of it different?
9. Please send the exact **Zeffy link for membership**.
10. In your own words, **what does membership money pay for?** (For example construction, equipment, launch, field operations, outreach.)
11. **What happens after the first 12 months?** Does membership continue, at what price, or does it end?

**C. Donations**

12. Please send the exact **Zeffy link for one-time donations**.
13. Is the **equipment registry** (or "sponsor the build") page still active? If so, what is its link? Which equipment has already been donated, so we do not ask for it again?

**D. Partners and trust**

14. Who are the current **local business partners**? Please send each one's name, logo, website link, and one sentence about them.
15. Have **RGB Graphic Solutions** and **Blue Wave Radio** agreed to have their names and logos on the site?
16. **Worldwide Veterinary Service** and **Mission Rabies**: what exact wording do they approve for describing the relationship (training? inspiration? partnership?), and may we show their logos? I will not use anything stronger than what they approve.
17. Please send the exact **501(c)(3) wording** you want shown (and the EIN, if you want it shown).
18. Is ROAR registered as a **Honduran NGO**? If yes, what is the exact wording?
19. Do you have any real **quotes or testimonials** from members, partners, or vets that we may use?

**E. The mobile unit and the team**

20. Is the launch team still **one full-time bilingual Honduran veterinarian, one full-time vet technician, and two part-time assistants**?
21. What is **confirmed versus still planned** for the unit itself: the vehicle, surgery area, recovery area, climate control, power (generator, solar, batteries), and water and sanitation? I only describe what you confirm.
22. Where does the build stand? For example: design, fundraising, vehicle purchase, build, equipment, staffing, launch. Please give each a status (done, in progress, planned) and a date if you have one.
23. The current FAQ makes specific claims I do not want to change without your approval. Please send short, accurate answers to these:
    - What happens after surgery, and where do animals recover?
    - What happens with critically ill animals?
    - Can people bring a sick animal, or a rescue?
    - Can volunteers work inside the unit?
    - How is money tracked?
    - Can members cancel monthly payments?
    - How will members get their shirt, and how does the membership card work?
    - How do local businesses take part in the perks?
24. The strategy page says the program targets **"around 70%" sterilization coverage** in each area. Can you confirm that figure and tell me where it comes from, so it is described accurately?

**F. Brand, photos, and contact**

25. **Logo files**, ideally SVG, plus PNG on a transparent background. Horizontal and stacked versions if you have them.
26. **Brand colors and fonts**, if there is a brand guide. If not, I will take them from the logo.
27. **Photos**, as large as you have them. The most useful: dogs and cats being treated, volunteers, the Honduran staff, communities and streets, any rendering or construction photos of the mobile unit, equipment, Founding Members, and partner businesses.
28. **Permission**: are the people in those photos okay with being shown on the site?
29. **Facebook and Instagram links.**
30. Is **info@roarmobile.org** monitored? **Who should receive messages from the website's contact form?**

**G. For launch**

31. **Who controls the roarmobile.org domain and its settings** (DNS)? I will need to point it away from Zibster. Please also tell me if the ROAR email addresses are hosted through Zibster, so we do not lose email.
32. Do you want privacy-friendly website statistics (a tool called Plausible, which does not track individuals)?
33. Does ROAR already have a **privacy policy and terms of use**? If not, I have written plain-language drafts for you to review.
34. Who can **review the Spanish text**? A native speaker, ideally someone on the ROAR team. I will send them a simple side-by-side sheet.

**Two things I would like you to check by eye**

- Does the first screen of the homepage explain ROAR Mobile to someone who has never heard of it?
- Is the wording about prevention and other rescues fair and kind? The site is written to add a layer of prevention, never to criticize other organizations.

Thank you. Once I have your answers (the money and links first, then photos and the logo), the site can go live shortly after.

Best,
Josh
